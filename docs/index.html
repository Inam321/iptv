Adding one to your TV

  1. SS IPTV (VIDAA) — gear icon, Content, External playlists, Add. Paste the link, save, then fully exit and reopen the app.
  2. Android TV — in TiviMate, IPTV Smarters or similar, add a new playlist and choose the M3U URL option.
  3. VLC — Ctrl+N, paste, play. Useful for testing a link before putting it on the TV.

Channel data comes from iptv-org, which indexes publicly available free-to-air streams. This project only verifies and re-groups them; it hosts no video.

Some streams are geo-restricted and will behave differently depending on where you are. Verification runs from GitHub's servers, so your results may vary slightly.
